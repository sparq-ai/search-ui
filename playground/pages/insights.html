Insights (search analytics)

The insights attribute makes the widget report searches and result clicks, each stamped with the search's queryId. Type, click a result, then press "Simulate order confirmation" — the purchase event rejoins the clicked item to its search. All events appear in the log instead of being sent (the tracking endpoint is stubbed on this page).

Click a result first — the purchase then carries that item's queryId. Without a click, the item ships unattributed (counts toward total revenue only).
