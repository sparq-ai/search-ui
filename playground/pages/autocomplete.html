Federated autocomplete

The search box above belongs to the "theme" header — <sparq-autocomplete input="#theme-search"> only attaches to it.

  • Focus the empty box → popular searches (the show-on="empty" section).
  • Type "nike" → products, categories, and pages sections — each with different markup from its own <template>.
  • Arrow keys traverse every section; Enter follows the active item; Enter with nothing active goes to the full search page.
  • Clicking a popular search fires sparq:ac-select (see the console).
